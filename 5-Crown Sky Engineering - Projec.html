01 — Overview

Twelve selected E&M works for financial institutions, data centers, commercial buildings, a hospital, a tunnel and railway stations.

The portfolio covers repair, maintenance, alteration and addition work, together with replacement of UPS, busduct, CCTV, backup generation, emergency lighting, escalators, typhoon shutters, main switchboards, AHUs and PAUs.

It also includes Durasteel and associated E&M works for a builders lift in a tunnel, and main E&M works for a hospital superstructure and refurbishment.
