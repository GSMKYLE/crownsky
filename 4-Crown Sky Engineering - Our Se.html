Built in the Greater Bay Area since 2013.

Crown Sky Engineering is the Hong Kong arm of a group founded in Macau in 2013. Our teams deliver MVAC, environmental control systems, maintenance and A&A works for airports, MTR, commercial towers and theme parks.

With in-house engineers and our own fabrication line, we take every project from early consultation, design and pricing through to construction, testing and long-term maintenance — guided by three principles:
