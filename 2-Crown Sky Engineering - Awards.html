Chapter 04 · People

Safe sites start with healthy minds.

Engineering is a demanding profession. These charters formalise how we look after the people behind every project — inclusion, mental health and a workplace where good moods are designed in.

  • A · Commendation

    Harmony at Workplace

    Recognises an inclusive, respectful culture where diverse teams collaborate without barriers.

  • B · Charter

    Good Mood @ Healthy Workplace

    A pledge to promote positive mental wellbeing, with resources that help staff manage pressure at work.

  • C · Certified

    Mental Health Friendly Organisation

    Working to remove stigma around mental health and build a caring, supportive environment.
