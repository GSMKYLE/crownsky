Kan Leung

MHKIS RPS Chartered Building Surveyor

Mr. Kan Leung is a Chartered Building Surveyor (MHKIS, RPS) with years of comprehensive experience delivering high-quality built environment projects. Possesses a proven track record in successfully managing the full project lifecycle of both minor alteration and addition (A&A) works and largescale new development projects. Combines strong technical expertise in building pathology and construction with advanced project management acumen. Expertly navigates the complexities of statutory regulations, securing necessary approvals and licenses. A strategic thinker skilled in balancing the needs of diverse stakeholders, from clients and design teams to contractors and regulatory bodies, ensuring projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest standards.

Keith Chow

RSO Environmental Officer BEAM Pro

Mr. Keith Chow is a registered safety practitioner (RSO) with expertise in engineering safety and management. He is well versed in health, safety and environmental legislation and regulations, with competencies in underground utilities detection, metal scaffold supervision, confined space operations, environmental management, rehabilitation management and fire safety. He is also a qualified Environmental Officer and BEAM Professional (BEAM Pro) for new buildings, existing buildings and data centres.
