Where It All Began

Canary Construction Limited

The Group was founded in Macau, launching its construction and engineering business and laying the foundation for future growth across the Greater Bay Area.

Macau
2014

Strengthening Manufacturing

Air-Conditioning Equipment Factory (Macau) Established

An in-house air-conditioning equipment factory was set up, boosting our ability to produce MVAC products independently and improving supply stability and quality control.

Manufacturing
2016

Entering Hong Kong

Canary (HK) Engineering Limited Established

The Group officially expanded into Hong Kong, taking on local MVAC and construction projects while building a local team and client network.

Hong Kong
2021

A Brand of Our Own

Crown Sky Engineering Limited Established

Officially incorporated in Hong Kong as an extension of the Group's Greater Bay Area business, specialising in MVAC engineering and Alteration & Addition (A&A) works.
