Our Operating Promise

The “Three Non-Guarantees”

Three simple rules every engineer, supervisor and subcontractor signs up to — backed by measurable controls on site.

  1. 01

    No cutting corners

    Work follows approved shop drawings, specifications and method statements — no unapproved substitutions.

    Material approval Inspection & test plans T&C records
  2. 02

    No delays

    Programmes are tracked weekly against milestones, with in-house ductwork fabrication in Zhuhai and Hong Kong securing supply.

    3-week look-ahead In-house fabrication Weekly progress reports
  3. 03

    No accidents

    An ISO 45001 occupational health and safety system applied on our sites.

    Permit-to-work ISO 45001:2018 Safety drills
